The story

We removed the room, not the ritual.

"A dining room tells you how to feel about the food. I'd rather the food do the talking."

Aarav Mehta spent nine years in fine-dining kitchens where the fire was the first thing lit each morning and the last thing watched each night. When he left, he kept exactly one thing: the hearth.

Ambar began as a single oak-fired oven in a rented industrial unit — no signage, no seats, one printed menu taped beside the pass. What it lacked in chandeliers it returned in attention: every plate cooked to order, timed to the minute a rider arrives.
